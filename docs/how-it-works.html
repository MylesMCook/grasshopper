A memory’s life

1

Start with context

Active confirmed preferences, applicable decisions and lessons, and a recent handoff load directly. Whole records are returned within a budget; omitted IDs are disclosed.

2

Save only what lasts

The agent saves explicit preferences, accepted decisions, verified lessons, or a concise handoff with a source. It does not mine transcripts or turn guesses into profile facts.

3

Correct without losing history

A correction names the record and expected revision. Concurrent edits conflict instead of silently replacing each other. Older revisions remain available for inspection and restoration.

4

Recall and recover

Scoped keyword and vector search finds current records; full get preserves qualifications. Archive is reversible. Consistent backups support migration. A post-write Go backup/reopen passed; a live service cutover remains open.
